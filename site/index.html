Link a stats page

Paste the game’s StatBroadcast link, or just its event number. This board opens the stats page in a new tab and fills in once that page shows the box score.

Find it on msubobcats.com → Schedule: the game’s “Stats” link.

  1. Accept & Continue on the stats page.
  2. Start the reader there: click your Pop board bookmark (it starts by itself if you use Tampermonkey). A small Pop board bar appears at the bottom-right.
  3. Put the windows side by side: stats page on one monitor, this board on the other.
